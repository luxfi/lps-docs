## Abstract

This LP specifies the integration of NIST-approved post-quantum cryptographic algorithms into the Lux Network.

## Motivation

Quantum computers threaten current cryptographic standards. Proactive migration ensures long-term security.

## Specification

### Algorithms

- **ML-KEM-768**: Key encapsulation (FIPS 203)
- **ML-DSA-65**: Digital signatures (FIPS 204)
- **SLH-DSA**: Hash-based signatures (FIPS 205)

### EVM Precompiles

| Address | Algorithm | Gas Cost |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| 0x0200 | ML-KEM | 3,000 |
| 0x0201 | ML-DSA | 5,000 |
| 0x0202 | SLH-DSA | 8,000 |

## Security Considerations

All algorithms are NIST-approved and quantum-resistant.
